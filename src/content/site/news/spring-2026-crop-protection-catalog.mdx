### Ready for the 2026 Spray Season

As soil temperatures rise and planting windows open, crop protection professionals across the hemisphere are preparing for the 2026 season. Here is your spring catalog preview.

**New Herbicide Formulations:**

- **SC Herbicide 240** — 240 g/L pre-emergence herbicide in suspension concentrate, offering extended residual control and improved tank-mix compatibility
- **WDG Herbicide 75** — 75% WDG post-emergence broadleaf herbicide with low dust granule technology for safer handling

**Insecticide Additions:**

- **SC Insecticide 200** — 200 g/L systemic insecticide in SC formulation with enhanced rainfastness for foliar application
- **FS Insecticide 350** — 350 g/L flowable seed treatment insecticide for early-season pest protection

**Fungicide Expansion:**

- **SC Fungicide 250** — 250 g/L broad-spectrum fungicide with preventive and curative activity, now available in SC for improved spray coverage
- **WDG Fungicide 80** — 80% WDG fungicide for powdery mildew and rust complexes, reformulated with improved dispersibility

**Registration Status:** Products listed are available for markets where registration has been granted. Contact your Agrospear representative for country-specific registration status and dossier availability.

Whether you are planning pre-emergence applications on cereals or foliar fungicide programs on fruits and vegetables, starting the season with the right formulation platform makes every application more effective and more economical.
