### New WDG Capacity at Agrospear

Agrospear is proud to announce the commissioning of a new water-dispersible granule (WDG) production line at our Qingdao manufacturing facility, significantly expanding capacity for herbicide and fungicide WDG formulations.

The new line features advanced fluidized-bed granulation equipment, upgraded sieving and classification systems, and integrated in-line particle size monitoring. This investment enables Agrospear to produce WDG formulations at higher throughput, tighter granule size distribution, and with improved dispersion performance — critical quality parameters that directly affect field application results.

**Key Capabilities:**

- **Fluidized-bed granulation** — Consistent granule formation with controlled moisture content below 2%, ensuring long shelf life and reliable dispersion
- **Expanded capacity** — Additional 3,000 tons per year of WDG output across herbicide and fungicide formulations
- **Tighter particle size control** — Integrated laser diffraction monitoring during production ensures D50 within 300–600 μm target range
- **Multiple WDG formulations available** — Including atrazine 80% WDG, mancozeb 80% WDG, propineb 70% WDG, and custom formulations under OEM
- **Pilot batches available** — 1–5 ton pilot orders for new WDG formulations, with full five-batch analysis and registration dossier support

### Why WDG Matters for Your Brand

Water-dispersible granules offer significant advantages over wettable powders (WP) and liquid formulations in many crop protection applications:

- **Dust-free handling** — Reduced operator exposure compared to WP, improving safety and compliance
- **Superior flowability** — Easy to measure and pour, no caking in storage
- **Longer shelf life** — No freezing risk (unlike SC formulations), stable at ambient temperature for 2+ years
- **Transport efficiency** — Higher AI concentration means lower freight cost per kilogram of active ingredient
- **Farmer preference** — Growing market demand for WDG over WP in Latin America, Africa, and Southeast Asia

### Quality Upgrades

The new production line incorporates several quality improvements:

- **In-line moisture monitoring** — Real-time moisture control during granulation and drying prevents over- or under-drying, ensuring consistent dispersion rate and suspensibility
- **Automated sieving** — Reduces fines and oversize particles, improving granule uniformity and flow
- **Integrated QC sampling** — Content analysis by HPLC, suspension rate (CIPAC MT 184), and dispersibility testing conducted on every batch
- **Stability data** — Accelerated stability testing (54°C, 14 days) and cold stability (0°C, 7 days) available for all new WDG formulations

### Available for OEM / Private Label

All WDG formulations produced on the new line are available for OEM and private label manufacturing. Agrospear provides:

- Complete five-batch analysis and registration documentation
- Custom concentration and adjuvant optimization
- GHS-compliant label design per your target market
- Custom packaging: 100g pouches through 25kg bags
