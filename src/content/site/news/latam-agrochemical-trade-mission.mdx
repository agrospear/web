### Building Bridges in Latin America

Agrospear is proud to announce its 2026 Latin America Agrochemical Trade Mission, a multi-country initiative connecting with distributors and registration partners across Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, and Mexico.

The mission introduces a Market Entry Partnership Program designed for regional agrochemical companies seeking OEM formulation partners — a program purpose-built for brands looking to expand their product portfolios with proven SC, WDG, and FS formulations from Agrospear's annual catalog.

The mission focuses on three strategic objectives:

- **Registration pathway mapping** — country-by-country analysis of dossier requirements, timeline expectations, and fast-track opportunities for reduced-risk formulations
- **Distributor partnership development** — structured meetings with pre-qualified regional distributors seeking reliable formulation suppliers
- **Market-specific formulation adaptation** — consultations on adjusting adjuvant systems and formulation parameters for tropical and subtropical cropping conditions

The trade mission takes place August 15-30, 2026, with scheduled stops in São Paulo, Buenos Aires, Bogotá, and Mexico City. Agrospear's regulatory affairs team and formulation specialists will accompany all meetings.

Registration for partnership meetings opens June 15 at agrospear.com/latam-mission. Agrospear will provide product samples and registration dossier summaries for all scheduled consultations.
