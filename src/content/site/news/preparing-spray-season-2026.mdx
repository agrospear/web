### Season-Ready in 30 Days

Peak spray season is approaching. Follow these planning steps to ensure your crop protection program delivers on time and on budget throughout 2026.

**Inventory Planning:** Audit current stock levels for all SC, WDG, and FS formulations. Cross-reference with projected application schedules for each crop and region. Order formulation batches 90–120 days before first application date to account for production lead time and shipping. Agrospear standard production runs require 45–60 days from order confirmation to shipment.

**Registration Milestones:** Verify registration status for all planned products in each target market. Renewal dossiers should be submitted 6–12 months before expiry. New product registrations in most markets require 18–36 months — plan 2027 launches now. Agrospear provides GLP study packages and regulatory documentation to support your dossier preparation.

**Formulation Orders:** Confirm batch specifications with your Agrospear account manager. Lock in annual volume pricing before the production calendar fills. Request stability data for any new formulation before committing to volume orders. Pilot batches (5–10 units) are available for field validation before full-scale commitment.

**Seasonal Demand Forecasting:** Pre-emergence herbicides peak in early spring; foliar insecticides and fungicides peak in mid-summer through early fall. Build 10–15% safety stock for top-selling formulations. Coordinate shipment schedules to avoid warehouse congestion during peak application windows.

**Storage and Handling:** Verify warehouse conditions meet formulation storage requirements — SC products above 5°C, WDG products below 30°C and below 70% relative humidity. Inspect container seals and labels on carry-over inventory before redistribution.

Following these steps will keep your crop protection program on track through the busiest spray season, whether you are deploying pre-emergence herbicides on cereals or foliar fungicides on specialty crops.
