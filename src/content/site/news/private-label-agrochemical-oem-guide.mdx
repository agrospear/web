### Why Private Label Agrochemical?

The global crop protection market exceeds $65 billion, driven by rising food demand, changing pest profiles, and the need for region-specific formulations. For entrepreneurs and existing agricultural distributors, private label agrochemical manufacturing offers the fastest path to market — no factory investment required, full control over branding and pricing.

Agrospear has helped launch 40+ agrochemical brands across Latin America, Africa, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East. Here's how the process works.

### Step 1: Market Research & Brand Positioning

Before contacting a manufacturer, define:

- **Target crops and pests**: Row crops, fruits and vegetables, or specialty crops? What are the primary pest, disease, or weed pressures?
- **Price tier**: Generic commodity formulations ($2–4/kg active), mid-range branded generics ($4–8/kg), or premium differentiated products ($8+/kg)?
- **Distribution**: Direct to farmer, through cooperatives, agricultural retailers, or government procurement programs?

Successful brands typically start with one crop segment (e.g., rice herbicides) and expand after establishing distribution.

### Step 3: Formulation Selection & Development

Your manufacturer should offer:

1. **Formulation catalog selection**: Choose from existing proven formulations (fastest, lowest cost) — AI, concentration, and formulation type already established with stability data
2. **Formulation customization**: Adjust concentration, adjuvant package, or formulation type to meet your market's specific needs
3. **Label and packaging design**: Regulatory-compliant label design per target market requirements (GHS classification, first-aid statements, precautionary language)
4. **Packaging specification**: Bottle, jerry can, or bag sizes; carton configuration; pallet specification for container loading

At Agrospear, our in-house R&D team handles everything from formulation optimization to final label artwork — included in the OEM package.

### Step 4: Sampling & Validation

Before mass production, a sample batch is produced for:

- **Quality verification**: Content analysis by HPLC/GC, physical properties (viscosity, pH, suspension rate, particle size for SC/WP/WDG), and packaging evaluation
- **Stability data**: Accelerated stability testing (54°C, 14 days) and cold stability testing to support registration dossiers
- **Registration support**: Five-batch analysis report, product specification, and COA — required for ICAMA, FAO/WHO, and most national registration authorities
- **Adjustments**: Fine-tune concentration, viscosity, or packaging before production commitment

### Step 5: Mass Production & Quality Control

During production, reputable factories follow:

- **ISO 9001:2015** quality management system
- **AQL 2.5** sampling standard per batch
- **Inline QC** at every production stage (raw material verification, formulation preparation, filling, sealing, labeling)
- **Final inspection** before packing: content analysis, physical properties check, label accuracy, and weight verification

### Step 6: Global Logistics & Delivery

Your manufacturer handles shipping to your warehouse or distributor network:

- **FOB Qingdao**: You arrange freight from the port; factory handles dangerous goods documentation and container packing
- **CIF major port**: Factory covers cost, insurance, and freight to your destination port, including DG sea freight compliance
- **DDP door-to-door**: Complete logistics including customs clearance and local delivery
- **Sea freight**: Standard for agrochemical shipments (IMDG Class 6.1 / Class 9 depending on product); 3–5 weeks to Latin America, 4–6 weeks to Africa, 2–3 weeks to Southeast Asia
