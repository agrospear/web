### CINA Bound

Agrospear will exhibit at CINA 2026, the China International Agrochemical Exhibition, October 15-17 at the Shanghai New International Expo Centre. This marks Agrospear's debut at China's premier agrochemical trade event.

The Agrospear booth (Hall 3, B-312) will feature the full formulation lineup with product displays and technical consultations:

- **SC Formulation Platform** — Suspension concentrates with D90 < 5μm and suspension rates > 90%
- **WDG Product Line** — Water-dispersible granules with low-dust technology and improved dispersibility
- **FS Seed Treatment Range** — Flowable seed treatment formulations for insecticide and fungicide applications
- **Green Formulation Initiative** — Water-based alternatives replacing solvent-based EC products
- **Co-Branding Program** — OEM partnership opportunities for custom-branded agrochemical products

Technical specialists will be available for one-on-one consultations on formulation development, registration support, and OEM manufacturing partnerships. Agrospear executives will also be available for B2B meetings with potential distributors and market-entry partners.

Visit agrospear.com/cina-2026 to schedule a meeting or book a technical consultation.
