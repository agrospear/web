### Streamlined Registration Across ASEAN Markets

Agrospear has entered into a partnership with the Southeast Asia Registration Corridor Initiative to provide multi-country registration support for agrochemical formulations across Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, and the Philippines.

The partnership covers streamlined dossier preparation aligned with the ASEAN harmonized registration framework, leveraging shared data packages across participating countries to reduce duplicate testing and accelerate time-to-market.

Key elements of the SEA Registration Corridor Partnership:

- **Unified GLP data package** — a single study set (acute toxicity, chronic toxicity, environmental fate, and residue trials) designed to meet the regulatory requirements of all four target markets
- **Country-specific dossier compilation** — tailored regulatory submissions addressing each nation's labeling, packaging, and field trial requirements
- **Regulatory timeline mapping** — projected approval timelines by country, with milestone tracking and proactive response management

The agreement includes discounted dossier preparation fees for corridor participants, dedicated regulatory affairs support from Agrospear's compliance team, and joint monitoring of application status across all four markets.

This partnership enables agrochemical brands to enter Southeast Asia's rapidly growing crop protection markets with a coordinated, cost-efficient registration strategy — reducing the typical 24–36 month registration timeline by an estimated 30% through dossier harmonization.
