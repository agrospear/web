## Broad-Spectrum Disease Control

Azoxystrobin is the world's most widely used strobilurin fungicide, delivering preventive and early-curative protection across an exceptionally wide range of crops and pathogens. Its systemic and translaminar activity ensures that both treated and newly emerging foliage remain protected, making it a cornerstone of integrated disease management programs.

### Mode of Action

Azoxystrobin inhibits mitochondrial respiration by binding to the Qo site of cytochrome bc1 (Complex III), blocking electron transport and halting ATP production in fungal cells. This QoI (Group 11) mode of action provides rapid spore germination inhibition and mycelial growth suppression across multiple pathogen groups.

### SC Formulation Advantages

- **Ultra-Fine Particle Size**: 1–5 µm particles ensure uniform spray coverage and rapid foliar uptake, even on waxy leaf surfaces
- **High Suspension Rate**: Exceeds 90% suspensibility in standard tank mixes, preventing nozzle blockage and ensuring consistent dose delivery
- **Zero Dust Handling**: Closed-transfer compatible for operator safety in large-scale operations
- **Easy Volumetric Dosing**: Liquid formulation enables precise measuring with standard graduated equipment

### Disease and Crop Spectrum

- **Cereals**: Wheat leaf rust, stripe rust, septoria blotch; barley net blotch, powdery mildew
- **Fruit**: Apple scab, grape downy mildew and powdery mildew, banana yellow sigatoka, citrus greasy spot
- **Vegetables**: Cucurbit powdery mildew, tomato early and late blight, pepper leaf spot, onion purple blotch
- **Other**: Rice blast, peanut leaf spot, soybean rust
