## Growth Management for High-Value Crops

Paclobutrazol is the leading gibberellin biosynthesis inhibitor for commercial fruit tree and ornamental crop management. By reducing endogenous gibberellin levels, it redirects plant resources from excessive vegetative growth toward reproductive development — improving flowering, fruit set, and harvest consistency in high-value orchard and plantation crops.

### Mode of Action

Paclobutrazol blocks the three-step oxidation of ent-kaurene to ent-kaurenoic acid catalyzed by cytochrome P450 monooxygenases in the gibberellin biosynthetic pathway. This reduces active gibberellin (GA1) concentrations, shortening internodes, compacting canopies, and promoting the transition from vegetative to reproductive growth.

### Application Methods

- **Foliar Spray**: 0.3–1.0 kg/ha for field crops, nursery stock, and young fruit trees — rapid uptake through leaf tissue for seasonal growth regulation
- **Soil Drench**: 0.5–2.0 g/tree applied to the root zone of mature fruit trees — delivers sustained uptake and multi-season effect with a single application
- **Trunk Injection**: Precision dosing for high-value orchard programs where uniform distribution and minimal environmental loss are required
