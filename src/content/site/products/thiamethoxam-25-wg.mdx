## Systemic Uptake & Long Residual

Thiamethoxam 25% WG is a systemic neonicotinoid insecticide that provides rapid uptake and extended residual control. After application, thiamethoxam is absorbed through leaves and roots, translocating acropetally to protect new growth. Its long residual activity reduces spray frequency, lowering application costs and improving operational efficiency for large-scale growers.

### WG Formulation Advantages

- **Dust-Free**: Water-dispersible granules eliminate dust during handling, protecting operator health and reducing product loss
- **Easy Measuring**: Free-flowing granules pour and dose accurately, eliminating the clumping and measurement errors common with wettable powders
- **Excellent Dispersion**: Granules disintegrate rapidly in spray water, forming a uniform suspension with no sediment or nozzle blockage
- **Storage Stability**: WG formulations resist moisture absorption and caking during storage, maintaining product integrity over the full shelf life

### Foliar & Soil Application

- **Foliar Spray**: Direct spray application for rapid knockdown of aphids, whitefly and leafhoppers at 0.1–0.2 kg/ha in vegetables and cotton
- **Soil-Directed**: In-furrow or banded soil application for systemic uptake through roots, protecting young plants during establishment at 0.2–0.3 kg/ha
- **Chemigation**: Compatible with drip and sprinkler irrigation systems for uniform delivery in intensively managed crops
- **Seed-Piece Treatment**: Potato seed-piece treatment for early-season flea beetle and aphid control
