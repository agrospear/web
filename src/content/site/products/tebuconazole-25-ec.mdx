## Protective and Curative Action

Tebuconazole is one of the most widely deployed triazole fungicides globally, recognized for its strong curative activity against established infections. Unlike purely protective fungicides, tebuconazole penetrates plant tissue and halts fungal development post-infection, making it an essential tool when disease pressure exceeds preventive thresholds.

### Mode of Action

Tebuconazole inhibits sterol 14α-demethylase (CYP51), blocking the conversion of lanosterol to ergosterol and disrupting fungal cell membrane integrity. This DMI (Group 3) mechanism stops mycelial growth and spore production, providing both protective and curative efficacy.

### EC Formulation Benefits

- **Superior Cuticular Penetration**: Emulsifiable concentrate spreads evenly on waxy leaf and fruit surfaces, maximizing uptake
- **Rapid Rainfastness**: Binds to plant tissue within 1–2 hours, maintaining efficacy after rainfall
- **Stable Tank Emulsion**: Forms uniform milky emulsion in water for consistent spray delivery
- **Excellent Shelf Stability**: Two-year shelf life with no phase separation under standard storage conditions

### Application Across Crops

- **Cereals**: Wheat leaf and stripe rust, septoria tritici blotch, loose smut; barley net blotch, powdery mildew, covered smut
- **Fruit Trees**: Apple and pear scab, stone fruit leaf curl and brown rot, grape powdery mildew
- **Vegetables**: Tomato early blight and leaf spot, cucurbit powdery mildew, pepper anthracnose
- **Other**: Peanut leaf spot, soybean rust, rice sheath blight
