## Rapid Knockdown Contact Action

Lambda-cyhalothrin 2.5% EC is a fast-acting pyrethroid insecticide that delivers rapid knockdown through contact and stomach action. Its sodium channel modulation disrupts nerve signal transmission in target insects, causing immediate cessation of feeding and rapid mortality — critical for protecting crops during peak pest pressure windows.

### EC Formulation Advantages

- **Instant Emulsification**: Forms a stable emulsion on contact with water for reliable, uniform spray application
- **Enhanced Penetration**: EC carrier system facilitates rapid uptake through the insect cuticle for faster knockdown
- **Residual Activity**: Formulated for sustained efficacy on treated surfaces, reducing the need for frequent re-applications
- **Tank-Mix Flexibility**: Compatible with a wide range of fungicides, insecticides and foliar fertilizers

### Application Scenarios

- **Cotton**: Bollworm, aphid and whitefly control at 0.5–0.8 L/ha — critical during squaring and boll development
- **Vegetables**: Diamondback moth, armyworm and aphid management in brassicas, solanaceous and cucurbit crops at 0.3–0.6 L/ha
- **Cereals**: Aphid and cutworm suppression in wheat and barley at 0.3–0.5 L/ha
- **Public Health**: Mosquito and vector control in residential and peri-urban environments

### Who It Is For

- Crop protection companies seeking a fast-acting pyrethroid for their branded product lines
- Brand owners targeting cotton and vegetable markets who need reliable knockdown performance
- Public health procurement agencies requiring vector control formulations with full documentation
