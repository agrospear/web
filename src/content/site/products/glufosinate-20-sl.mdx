## Fast Burndown Activity

Glufosinate-ammonium 20% SL delivers rapid, visible weed desiccation within 2–5 days of application. Its partial-systemic mode of action — inhibition of glutamine synthetase — causes ammonia accumulation in plant cells, leading to photorespiratory stress and rapid tissue necrosis. This fast-acting profile makes it the preferred choice for situations where quick burndown is essential: orchard management, pre-plant field preparation, and inter-row weed control in high-value crops.

## SL Formulation Benefits

The soluble liquid formulation provides operational advantages for professional applicators:

- **Rapid tank preparation** — dissolves instantly in water with no pre-mixing required
- **Uniform spray distribution** — consistent droplet formation for thorough coverage
- **Excellent storage stability** — no sediment, no crystallization, 2-year shelf life guaranteed
- **Adjuvant compatibility** — pairs effectively with surfactants and drift-control agents for optimized performance

## Application Scenarios

Glufosinate 20% SL is versatile across multiple cropping and non-crop environments:

- **Orchards** — strip treatment along tree rows for weed-free management zones
- **Vineyards** — under-vine burndown without root uptake concerns in established vines
- **Vegetable fields** — pre-plant and stale seedbed preparation before transplanting
- **Non-crop areas** — industrial sites, railways, irrigation ditches, and rights-of-way where rapid vegetation knockdown is required
