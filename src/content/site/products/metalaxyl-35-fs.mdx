## Seedling Disease Protection from Day One

Metalaxyl-M 35% FS delivers systemic oomycete protection from the moment the seed is planted. By coating the seed with a precise, uniform film of the R-enantiomer active, it creates a protective zone around the germinating seedling — defending against Pythium, Phytophthora and downy mildew during the critical emergence window when seedlings are most vulnerable.

### Mode of Action

Metalaxyl-M inhibits RNA polymerase I in oomycete pathogens, blocking ribosomal RNA synthesis and halting protein production. This Group 4 mechanism is highly specific to oomycetes, providing targeted protection against Pythium, Phytophthora and downy mildew without disrupting beneficial soil microorganisms.

### FS Formulation Advantages

- **Optimized Viscosity**: Engineered for uniform seed coating with commercial treatment equipment — no runs, drips, or uneven patches
- **Uniform Coating**: Precise film formation ensures every seed receives the target dose for consistent stand establishment
- **Bright Color for Visibility**: Integrated colorant provides instant visual confirmation that seeds have been treated, preventing mixing errors and ensuring quality control
- **Flowable Convenience**: Ready-to-use liquid eliminates dust and handling hazards associated with powder seed treatments

### Seed Treatment Application

- **Application Rate**: 100–300 mL per 100 kg seed, adjusted by crop and disease pressure
- **Equipment Compatibility**: Designed for standard commercial seed-treatment machines (batch and continuous)
- **Tank-Mix Partners**: Compatible with fludioxonil, thiram, mancozeb and insecticide seed treatments for comprehensive seedling protection packages
- **Drying Time**: Rapid drying on seed surface for immediate bagging and handling
