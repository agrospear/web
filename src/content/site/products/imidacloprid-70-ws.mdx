## Early-Season Protection from Day One

Imidacloprid 70% WS delivers systemic insect protection from the moment the seed is planted. As a high-concentration water-dispersible powder, it is the preferred seed treatment formulation for OEM partners targeting cereal, corn and cotton markets where early-season pest pressure determines yield outcomes.

### WS Formulation Advantages

- **Easy Slurry Preparation**: WS powder disperses rapidly in water, forming a homogeneous slurry ready for commercial seed treaters
- **Uniform Coating**: Fine particle size ensures even distribution across every seed, eliminating hot spots and coverage gaps
- **Good Adhesion**: Formulated adjuvants bind the active ingredient firmly to the seed coat, resisting abrasion during handling and planting
- **No Dust**: Low-dust formulation minimizes operator exposure and meets modern handling safety standards

### Seed Treatment Application

- **Slurry Treaters**: Compatible with all major commercial slurry seed treatment equipment — flow-through and batch systems
- **Film Coating**: Can be applied as a film coat with polymer binders for enhanced seed flow and visibility
- **Dosage Per Crop**: Wheat 200–300 g/100 kg seed, corn 300–500 g/100 kg seed, cotton 400–500 g/100 kg seed — adjust per local registration
