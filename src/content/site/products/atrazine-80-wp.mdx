## Pre-Emergence Weed Control in Corn

Atrazine 80% WP provides reliable, residual pre-emergence control of the annual broadleaf and grass weeds that compete with corn during its critical early growth stages. By inhibiting Photosystem II in germinating weed seedlings, atrazine prevents photosynthetic energy production before emerged weeds can establish. Applied pre-emergence or early post-emergence, it creates a protective weed-free window that allows corn to develop an optimal canopy without early-season competition.

## WP Formulation Advantages

The wettable powder formulation offers practical and economic benefits:

- **High active ingredient loading** — 80% concentration reduces transport and storage costs per unit of active
- **Excellent suspensibility** — uniform particle dispersion in spray tank for consistent field application
- **Long residual activity** — soil-persistent action provides extended weed control through the critical period
- **Cost-effective** — proven chemistry with established manufacturing efficiency translates to competitive per-hectare costs

## Application Timing

- **Pre-emergence** — apply to prepared soil surface after planting but before crop or weed emergence for maximum residual benefit
- **Early post-emergence** — can be applied to corn up to the 5-leaf stage for control of emerged annual weeds; crop safety is excellent at labeled rates
- **Tank-mix programs** — commonly combined with acetochlor or s-metolachlor for expanded grass and broadleaf spectrum in a single pass
