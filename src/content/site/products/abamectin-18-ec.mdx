## Broad-Spectrum Insect & Mite Control

Abamectin 1.8% EC is a proven broad-spectrum insecticide and acaricide trusted by growers worldwide. Its unique mode of action — activating GABA and glutamate-gated chloride channels — delivers effective control of mites, leafminers and lepidopteran pests that have developed resistance to other chemical classes.

### EC Formulation Benefits

- **Rapid Emulsification**: EC formulation emulsifies instantly in spray water, forming a stable milky emulsion for uniform application
- **Excellent Crop Coverage**: Low surface tension enables thorough coverage of leaf surfaces including undersides where mites congregate
- **Translaminar Activity**: Abamectin moves into leaf tissue, providing residual control of feeding pests while maintaining low surface residue
- **Tank-Mix Compatibility**: Compatible with commonly used fungicides and insecticides for integrated spray programs

### Application Across Crops

- **Vegetables**: Tomatoes, peppers, cucurbits — control spider mites, leafminers and diamondback moth at 0.5–1.0 L/ha
- **Fruit Trees**: Apples, citrus, grapes — suppress tetranychid and eriophyid mites at 1.0–1.5 L/ha
- **Cotton**: Spider mite and bollworm complex management in cotton at 0.8–1.2 L/ha
- **Ornamentals**: Greenhouse and nursery mite and thrips control
