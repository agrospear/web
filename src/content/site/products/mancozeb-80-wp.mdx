## Multi-Site Resistance Management

Mancozeb is the cornerstone of resistance management strategies worldwide. Its multi-site mode of action — reacting with thiol groups across multiple fungal enzymes — makes resistance development virtually impossible. As single-site fungicides face increasing resistance pressure, mancozeb remains the most reliable mixing partner and standalone protectant.

### Mode of Action

Mancozeb interferes with fungal enzyme systems by reacting with sulfhydryl (–SH) groups on proteins, disrupting multiple metabolic processes simultaneously. This multi-site mechanism (FRAC Group M3) means that no single gene mutation can confer resistance, preserving long-term field efficacy.

### WP Formulation Advantages

- **Proven Cost-Effectiveness**: The most economical mancozeb formulation for large-scale field programs
- **Excellent Wetting and Adhesion**: Fine WP particles distribute uniformly on leaf surfaces with standard wetting agents
- **Flexible Tank-Mix Compatibility**: Stable in mixture with most systemic fungicides (triazoles, strobilurins, phenylamides)
- **Established Supply Chain**: Globally recognized formulation with straightforward regulatory pathways

### Disease and Crop Spectrum

- **Potatoes**: Late blight (Phytophthora infestans), early blight (Alternaria solani)
- **Tomatoes**: Early blight, septoria leaf spot, tomato blight complex
- **Grapes**: Downy mildew (Plasmopara viticola)
- **Vegetables**: Downy mildew and anthracnose on cucurbits, peppers, onions and crucifers
- **Other**: Peanut leaf spot, apple scab (protective), citrus melanose
