## Pre-Emergence Grass Weed Control

Acetochlor 90% EC is a leading pre-emergence herbicide for annual grass weed management in corn and soybean production. By inhibiting very long-chain fatty acid synthesis in germinating weed seedlings, acetochlor prevents cell division and early seedling development — stopping weeds before they emerge. This early-season weed-free environment is critical for crop establishment, as even brief periods of grass competition during the first 3–4 weeks can cause significant yield reduction in both corn and soybeans.

## EC Formulation Advantages

The emulsifiable concentrate formulation provides proven operational benefits:

- **High active concentration** — 90% loading reduces logistics costs and storage requirements per unit of active ingredient
- **Uniform emulsification** — forms a stable oil-in-water emulsion in the spray tank for consistent application
- **Excellent soil activity** — readily adsorbs to soil colloids for reliable pre-emergence weed control across varying soil types
- **Tank-mix compatible** — frequently combined with atrazine (corn) or metribuzin (soybeans) for one-pass broadleaf and grass control

## Crop Compatibility

Acetochlor is one of the few pre-emergence grass herbicides with dual crop registration:

- **Corn** — applied pre-emergence or early pre-plant for control of foxtails, barnyardgrass, crabgrass, and fall panicum; selectivity is provided by rapid crop metabolism
- **Soybeans** — applied pre-emergence for annual grass control plus suppression of small-seed broadleaves like lambsquarters and pigweed; safe across all soybean maturity groups at labeled rates
- **Rotational flexibility** — short soil residual allows planting of rotational crops within normal rotation intervals
