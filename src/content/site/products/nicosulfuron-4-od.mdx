## Post-Emergence Grass Control in Corn

Nicosulfuron 4% OD is the benchmark post-emergence herbicide for grass weed control in corn. Its ALS-inhibiting mode of action halts cell division in growing points of susceptible grasses, causing growth cessation within 24–48 hours and visible chlorosis and necrosis within 7–14 days. Nicosulfuron controls the grass weeds that escape pre-emergence programs — shattercane, foxtails, barnyardgrass, and johnsongrass — while simultaneously suppressing key broadleaf species such as pigweed and velvetleaf.

## OD Formulation Benefits

The oil dispersion formulation delivers distinct agronomic and application advantages:

- **Reduced drift** — larger, oil-based droplets minimize off-target movement compared to water-based formulations
- **Rain-fastness** — spray deposit adheres to leaf surfaces within 1 hour; efficacy is retained after rainfall events
- **Enhanced uptake** — oil carrier improves penetration through the waxy cuticle of grass weeds, accelerating translocation to meristems
- **Crop safety** — corn metabolizes nicosulfuron rapidly through cytochrome P450 pathways, providing excellent selectivity across hybrid types

## Application Window

- **Optimal timing** — apply when grass weeds are 3–5 leaf stage and corn is between the 3- and 8-leaf stage
- **Early post-emergence** — effective on small, actively growing weeds; larger weeds may require the upper end of the rate range
- **Sequential programs** — can follow pre-emergence atrazine or acetochlor programs to clean up grass weed escapes
- **Tank-mix flexibility** — compatible with bromoxynil, dicamba, and other broadleaf herbicides for one-pass weed control
