## Broad-Spectrum Weed Control

Glyphosate 41% SL delivers reliable, non-selective control across a wide range of annual and perennial weeds. Its systemic action ensures translocation from treated foliage to underground rhizomes and root systems, achieving complete weed kill rather than top-growth suppression alone. This makes it the cornerstone herbicide for pre-planting field preparation, fallow management, and non-crop vegetation control.

## Formulation Advantages

The soluble liquid formulation offers distinct practical benefits for distributors and applicators:

- **No sediment or precipitation** — homogeneous solution remains stable in tank and storage
- **Easy mixing** — dissolves readily in water without requiring pre-mixing or agitation
- **Stable storage** — 2-year shelf life at ambient temperature with no crystallization risk
- **Tank-mix compatible** — suitable for co-application with adjuvants and other herbicides

## Application Timing

Glyphosate 41% SL fits multiple application windows across the cropping cycle:

- **Pre-planting** — burndown of existing vegetation before crop seeding or transplanting
- **Pre-emergence** — treatment of emerged weeds prior to crop emergence
- **Post-emergence** — directed or shielded application in established crops with appropriate selectivity measures
- **Fallow and non-crop** — vegetation management on roadsides, irrigation channels, industrial sites, and pasture renovation
