Vegetable crops — tomatoes, potatoes, peppers, cucurbits and leafy greens — face constant disease pressure from fungal pathogens. Late blight, early blight, downy mildew and powdery mildew can destroy a crop within days under favorable conditions. A distributor serving vegetable growers must supply a fungicide portfolio that combines protective multi-site chemistry (for resistance management and broad-spectrum coverage) with curative systemic chemistry (for disease that has already established), plus seed treatment options for seedling protection from day one.

## Vegetable fungicide portfolio strategy

The vegetable fungicide market divides into three functional tiers. A complete portfolio addresses all three:

**Protective fungicides (multi-site)** — Multi-site fungicides interfere with multiple metabolic processes simultaneously, making resistance development extremely unlikely. They are the foundation of every vegetable spray program:

- **Mancozeb 80% WP** — the world's most widely used protective fungicide. Multi-site action against late blight, early blight, downy mildew and Septoria leaf spot. Low cost per application, no resistance risk, and the anchor product for any vegetable fungicide portfolio. Mancozeb is also the ideal tank-mix partner for systemic fungicides, providing multi-site protection alongside single-site curative action
- **Mancozeb 80% WDG** — dust-free granule alternative for markets where WP dust exposure is a regulatory concern. Same multi-site efficacy, safer handling

**Curative fungicides (systemic, single-site)** — Systemic fungicides are absorbed and translocated within the plant, providing curative activity against established infections and rainfast protection:

- **Azoxystrobin 25% SC** — strobilurin systemic fungicide with broad-spectrum activity against downy mildew, powdery mildew, early blight and rust. Translaminar and mesophyll movement protect new growth. The premium curative SKU for vegetable portfolios, commanding higher margins and building technical credibility with progressive growers
- **Metalaxyl-M + Mancozeb 68% WP** — combination of systemic (metalaxyl-M targets oomycetes from within) and multi-site (mancozeb protects the surface). The standard for late blight and downy mildew control in potatoes and tomatoes, combining curative and protective action in a single application

**Seed treatment** — Seed and seedling protection from the first day:

- **Metalaxyl-M 35% FS** — flowable seed treatment concentrate for the control of seedling damping-off and early-season oomycete diseases. Applied to seed before planting, it provides systemic protection during the critical emergence period. Essential for potato seed piece treatment and tomato transplant programs

## Combination approach for complete spray programs

No single fungicide product controls all vegetable diseases. Professional spray programs combine protective and curative chemistry in a structured sequence:

1. **Early season** — mancozeb WP protective sprays at 7–10 day intervals, establishing baseline protection before disease pressure builds
2. **Mid-season** — alternate mancozeb protective sprays with azoxystrobin SC curative applications at disease onset, maintaining multi-site coverage while adding systemic curative activity
3. **Late season** — metalaxyl-M + mancozeb WP combination applications if late blight or downy mildew threatens, leveraging both systemic and multi-site action
4. **Seed/seedling** — metalaxyl-M FS seed treatment provides the first 3–4 weeks of protection, reducing early spray applications

This program structure ensures that no single-site fungicide is applied more than twice in sequence, maintaining resistance management discipline while delivering complete disease control through the season.
