Suspension Concentrate (SC) is the most widely used modern agrochemical formulation type worldwide. The active ingredient is milled to fine solid particles and suspended in a water-based carrier with dispersants, wetting agents and thickeners, producing a stable, pourable suspension that mixes readily with water for spray application.

## How SC formulation works

The active ingredient is reduced to particles with a D90 below 5 μm through wet milling in a bead mill or sand mill. These fine particles are then stabilized in water using a carefully balanced system of dispersants (to prevent settling and agglomeration), thickeners (to control viscosity and prevent hard packing), and wetting agents (to ensure rapid dispersion when the farmer adds the product to the spray tank). Antifoam agents and preservatives round out the formulation.

## Benefits of SC formulations

SC formulations combine the performance advantages of fine-particle application with the handling safety of a water-based system:

- **No dust** — liquid formulation eliminates operator exposure to airborne active ingredient during measuring and pouring
- **Easy to measure and pour** — calibrated viscosity and no caking mean accurate dosing straight from the bottle
- **High bio-efficacy** — sub-5 μm particle size gives excellent leaf coverage and uptake, often outperforming WP at the same dose
- **Safe handling** — water-based carrier replaces organic solvents, reducing toxicity and flammability risks
- **Environmentally preferable** — lower VOC content than EC formulations, no solvent waste
