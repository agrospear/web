Water-Dispersible Granule (WDG), also known as Dry Flowable (DF), is a dust-free granular formulation that disintegrates and disperses rapidly when added to water in the spray tank. WDG combines the storage and handling advantages of a dry product with the application performance of a liquid suspension, making it the preferred solid formulation for modern agriculture.

## How WDG formulation works

The active ingredient is blended with dispersants, wetting agents, binders and disintegrants, then processed through wet granulation (fluid-bed or high-shear) or extrusion to form uniform granules in the 0.5–2.0 mm range. The granule structure is designed to break apart rapidly on contact with water — disintegrants swell and fracture the granule, dispersants suspend the released particles, and wetting agents ensure the particles are wetted and distributed throughout the spray volume.

## Benefits of WDG formulations

WDG formulations address the key limitations of wettable powders while retaining their storage advantages:

- **Dust-free** — granules eliminate the inhalation hazard and operator exposure associated with WP powders, meeting increasingly strict regulatory requirements
- **Easy handling** — granules flow freely, do not cake, and can be measured by volume or weight without dust or spillage
- **Precise dosing** — uniform granule size and consistent bulk density allow accurate volumetric dosing
- **Good shelf stability** — dry granules resist microbial growth and chemical degradation better than water-based SC formulations, particularly in tropical storage conditions
- **Reduced packaging volume** — higher bulk density than WP means smaller, lighter packaging per kilogram of active ingredient

## Production methods

Agrospear employs two WDG production routes depending on the active ingredient and customer specification:

**Wet granulation (fluid-bed)** — the active ingredient and formulation aids are suspended in a fluid-bed granulator and sprayed with a binder solution. The resulting granules are spherical, free-flowing and disintegrate rapidly. This method is preferred for high-value active ingredients where uniform granule size is critical.

**Extrusion granulation** — the active ingredient blend is wetted with a binder solution, extruded through a die, and spheronized or cut to length. Extrusion is cost-effective for higher-volume products and achieves excellent hardness and low friability.
